Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-05-09

This Privacy Policy explains how Dronefeed collects, uses, stores and protects information when you use the Dronefeed Android app.

1. Who we are

Dronefeed is a community app for FPV/LOS drone pilots and drone content. You can contact us at dronefeed.app@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the app, Dronefeed may collect and store the following information:

  • Account information: email address, user ID, username, display name, password authentication state and basic account settings.
  • Profile information: avatar, bio, pilot type, drone model, home spot/location text, creator status and public profile information you choose to add.
  • User content: posts, images, videos, captions, tags, comments, ratings, likes, favorites and spot submissions.
  • Location-related content: spot names, cities, countries, coordinates and user-entered spot details. Dronefeed spot data is intended for community spot discovery and does not replace official aviation, geozone or local law checks.
  • Messages and safety data: direct messages, read status/receipt metadata, blocked users, reports, bug reports and moderation-related records.
  • Private user data: private spot notes and private spots if you use those features.
  • Technical data: data required to operate Firebase services, store media, secure accounts and keep the app functional.

3. How we use information

We use information to:

  • create and manage user accounts;
  • show profiles, posts, comments, spots and community content;
  • provide chat, follow, favorites, private notes and private spots;
  • store uploaded images and videos;
  • moderate content, process reports and protect users from abuse;
  • debug problems and improve reliability;
  • comply with legal, safety and platform requirements.

4. Firebase and service providers

Dronefeed uses Google Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Firebase Storage. These services help us authenticate users, store app data and store uploaded media. Google may process technical information as a service provider according to its own terms and policies.

5. Sharing of information

Dronefeed does not sell personal information. We may share or make available information in the following limited ways:

  • public profile and public community content may be visible to other Dronefeed users;
  • messages are visible to the conversation participants;
  • reports and moderation data may be reviewed by authorized staff;
  • service providers such as Firebase process data needed to operate the app;
  • we may disclose information if required by law or to protect users, the app or others.

6. User-generated content

Dronefeed includes user-generated content such as posts, comments, media, spots and messages. Do not upload content you do not have the right to share. Do not share sensitive personal information of other people.

7. Data retention and account deletion

You can request account and data deletion. See Account and Data Deletion.

When an account is deleted, Dronefeed may anonymize the account identity and keep some public community content, such as posts, comments, ratings or spots, in anonymized form to preserve community context and app integrity. Private account data and account-identifying information are deleted or anonymized where technically and legally possible.

8. Security

We use Firebase security rules and app-level safeguards to protect user data. No system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, but we work to limit access to data based on the user, feature and moderation role.

9. Children and age restriction

Dronefeed is intended for users aged 18 and older. It is not designed for children or minors.

10. Your choices

You can update profile information in the app, manage some privacy and chat settings, block users, report content and request deletion of your account and associated personal data.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app changes, when legal requirements change or when platform requirements change. The latest version will be available on this page.

12. Contact

For privacy questions, contact dronefeed.app@gmail.com.
