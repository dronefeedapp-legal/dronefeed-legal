Account and Data Deletion

Last updated: 2026-05-09

This page explains how Dronefeed users can request deletion of their app account and associated data.

1. Delete your account in the app

If you can access your account, use the in-app deletion path:

  1. Open the Dronefeed app.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Open Account & security.
  4. Choose the account deletion option and follow the instructions.

2. Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, email us from the email address linked to your Dronefeed account:

dronefeed.app@gmail.com

Please include:

  • Subject: Dronefeed account deletion request
  • Your Dronefeed username, if known
  • The email address used for the Dronefeed account
  • A short note that you want your Dronefeed account deleted

3. What is deleted or anonymized

When your account deletion is processed, Dronefeed deletes or anonymizes account-identifying information where technically and legally possible. This may include account profile data, email address, avatar/profile information, private notes, private spots and account settings.

4. Community content

Dronefeed may keep some public community content, such as posts, spots, comments, ratings or messages, in anonymized form to preserve community context, safety records and app integrity. The visible account identity may be replaced with a generic deleted-user identity such as Fly Away.

5. Timing

We aim to process deletion requests within a reasonable time. We may ask for additional verification if the request does not come from the account email or if we need to prevent unauthorized deletion.

6. Contact

For account deletion requests or questions, contact dronefeed.app@gmail.com.

Contact: dronefeed.app@gmail.com
